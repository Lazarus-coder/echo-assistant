01

Your model, with a clear path for your data

Conversations, memory, and execution records are stored on your device. Content used to generate responses is sent to your configured model; search queries go to your selected search service.

02

Separate controls for research, screen, and tools

Background research, screen awareness, companion mode, and workspace tools start off. Selecting the deep memory profile also enables screen awareness. OCR text or images may be sent to your model; capture can be paused at any time.

03

A way to continue, and a way to stop

Pause or cancel goals, and manage or delete memories. Quiet hours and frequency limits help reduce interruptions. Activity records are available for review.

04

Approval before execution. Records afterward.

Shell and MCP calls require approval. Shell runs with your local account's privileges, without an operating-system sandbox. Stopping a run cannot undo actions that have already completed.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you begin,
a little clarity.

How do I know setup is complete?

Open chat from the Echo menu-bar icon, check your model connection in the setup wizard, and send a short message. If the window does not open or you cannot get a reply, follow the troubleshooting steps to check service status and logs.

Do I need an Echo account or model credits?

Echo does not require an account. You supply your own model endpoint and credentials. Model and paid search usage are billed by the providers you choose. Available features depend on what your endpoint supports.

Does local-first mean fully offline?

No. Conversations and memory are stored locally, but responses use your configured model, and online research accesses external sources. If you connect a compatible local model, check each tool's network requirements separately.

Will Echo automatically read my email, browser, or calendar?

Echo does not currently connect to these apps automatically. Research uses new Echo conversations, public sources you share, attached documents, and selected folders. Screen awareness is a separate, optional capability.

Will Echo keep following up when my Mac is closed?

No. Checks stop when your Mac sleeps or Echo is not running. Overdue checks resume when it runs again. Review research results yourself, and keep independent reminders for important commitments.

Is Echo ready for production use?

Echo is still in alpha, intended for early users willing to share feedback. Research quality has not yet been evaluated in real-world use. By default, the service listens on your local network without a password. Enable a password or restrict the listening address before using it on shared or untrusted networks.
